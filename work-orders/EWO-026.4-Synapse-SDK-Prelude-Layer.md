## 1. Purpose

`EWO-026.3` completed the Ergonomics Layer. This work order populates the Curated Entry Layer (`prelude`) — `ARCH-014` §5, item 3 — with a 22-item curated subset of Foundation and Ergonomics, selected against a five-criterion Curation Test, every inclusion and exclusion individually justified, empirically proving Hello Durable World (`ARCH-014` §15) is achievable through Prelude alone.

---

## 2. Architectural Authority

Every included item traces to `ARCH-014` §5 item 3's corrected sourcing rule (v0.6.0/v0.7.0): Curated Entry may draw directly or transitively from Foundation and Ergonomics alike, narrowing only, never adding capability. `ARCH-014` §9's disclosed, temporary constitutional expectation governs Stable-tier judgment for items not yet covered by a formal tier-marking mechanism (`ARCH-014` §19, still deferred). `ARCH-014` §15's Hello Durable World requirement is the concrete, testable criterion this work order's own integration tests (`sdk/tests/prelude.rs`) satisfy directly.

---

## 3. Objectives

1. Select and re-export a curated subset of Foundation (Runtime entry/outcome, Actor authoring, Capability/bootstrap grants, Durability) sufficient, alone, to achieve Hello Durable World.
2. Select and re-export the three Ergonomics composition functions (`message`, `bootstrap_grant`, `define_and_create`), disclosing their comparatively young, single-test-suite evidentiary basis honestly rather than overstating it.
3. Individually justify every excluded `synapse_common` item against the five-criterion Curation Test, including `Provenance` (added at Amendment v0.2.0, Finding F01).
4. Prove, via integration test, that Hello Durable World and ordinary messaging-plus-capability-denial are both reachable through Prelude alone, with no Foundation or Ergonomics import required.

---

## 4. Non-Goals

This work order MUST NOT, and its implementation does not:

- introduce any capability, macro, builder, or behavior Foundation or Ergonomics does not already provide;
- populate `extension`;
- modify `foundation.rs`, `ergonomics.rs`, or any Runtime, Persistence, Recovery, Scheduling, or Capability behavior;
- introduce a formal Public API tier-marking mechanism (`ARCH-014` §19 remains deferred);
- resolve `ARCH-014` Observation `OBS-01`, left for a future, separately authorized documentation-maintenance work item.

## 7. Engineering Amendment Record — Findings F01, F02

An Independent Engineering Review of the original implementation found two Minor findings:

- **F01** — the module's own exclusion audit of `synapse_common` omitted `Provenance`, a zero-field unit struct, without stating why it was excluded, leaving the audit's own claim of completeness unsupported for that one item.
- **F02** — the three re-exported Ergonomics functions were treated as Stable tier without disclosing that, as of this writing, they are the newest items in the SDK, exercised only by their own dedicated test suite, and that no formal Public API tier-marking mechanism yet exists (`ARCH-014` §19) — their Stable-tier treatment here rests on review-chain completeness, not on the accumulated-usage evidence `ARCH-014` §8 otherwise ties Stable status to.

**Resolution** (Amendment v0.2.0), confined entirely to `sdk/src/prelude.rs`: a new bullet added to the `synapse_common` exclusion list naming `Provenance` and its own confirmed-unreferenced status as sufficient grounds for exclusion (F01); a disclosure note inserted immediately before the Ergonomics `pub use` line, stating the narrower evidentiary basis and the absence of a tier-marking mechanism explicitly (F02). No re-export added, removed, or changed. An Independent Engineering Re-Review independently re-verified both corrections directly against the repository (re-running the same `Provenance` reference search; independently re-tracing the Ergonomics test suite's own scope), confirmed zero regression, and concluded `READY FOR FOUNDER IMPLEMENTATION APPROVAL`.
